We founded Meethink because we kept seeing the same problems across organisations: processes that had grown too complex, technology that wasn't delivering, and teams spending enormous effort on work that should be streamlined. We built our careers solving exactly these problems — and now we do it independently, without the constraints of a vendor relationship.

The challenge

Life sciences events are complex — and no single tool was built to handle all of them.

Friction in Cross Functional Collaboration

Strategic events cut across medical, commercial, compliance, and finance teams. Without a shared coordination layer, each handoff creates risk — duplicated effort, missed approvals, and accountability gaps.

Technology that doesn't quite fit

Standard platforms are configured for field events at scale. Adapting them to strategic events requires expertise that vendor implementation teams rarely bring.

Compliance rules are country-specific, platform-specific, and constantly evolving. Without the right processes and tooling, organisations are exposed — often without knowing it.

Compliance Risk in Workflows

CRM infrastructure is regional by design. Strategic events aren't. The result is fragmented data, manual handoffs, and events that fall through the cracks.
